# EcoMap Technologies

> Map, connect, and measure your entrepreneurial ecosystem. 100+ ecosystems. 250,000+ resources mapped. Trusted nationwide.

EcoMap Technologies builds ecosystem intelligence software for economic development organizations, chambers of commerce, universities, arts agencies, and state and local governments. The platform turns scattered regional resource directories, referrals, and stakeholder relationships into a measurable, reportable network.

## Solutions
- [EcoMap Discover](https://ecomap.tech/solutions/discover): Resource hub & navigation. Describes the ecosystem-mapping platform and its features: collections, intelligent search, analytics and embeddable cards.
- [Ecosystem Relationship Manager (ERM)](https://ecomap.tech/solutions/ecosystem-relationship-manager): Ecosystem relationship mgmt. Explains how organizations can track relationships, visualize networks, identify gaps and manage referrals.
- [Ecosystem ChatBot](https://ecomap.tech/solutions/chatbot): AI-powered resource navigation. Introduces the AI assistant that answers questions and delivers relevant resources.
- [Economic Intelligence Monitor](https://ecomap.tech/solutions/economic-intelligence-monitor): Regional benchmarking. Benchmarks regional ecosystem performance with the Economic Intelligence Monitor for economic development leaders, funders, and policymakers.
- [Nonprofit Intelligence Dashboard](https://ecomap.tech/solutions/nonprofit-intelligence-dashboard): Nonprofit financial intelligence. Explains the Nonprofit Intelligence Dashboard for analyzing IRS financial data, sector health, funding gaps, and capacity trends across nonprofit networks.
- [Custom Services & Solutions](https://ecomap.tech/solutions/custom-services): Explains EcoMap custom services, reports, analysis, and multi-provider project support for ecosystem teams.
- [Solutions Overview](https://ecomap.tech/solutions): Brief introduction to EcoMap's platform and AI capabilities. Summarizes all products with links to individual product pages.
